## The short answer

For most studios and one-bedroom apartments, get the **Tapo RV30 Max Plus**. It empties
itself into a dock that takes just 9 × 6.5 in of floor, it did well on hard floors in
independent tests, and it has the highest Trust Score here (81).

If even that dock is too much, the **Roborock Q7 M5** charges on a dock barely bigger than
a phone (6.3 × 2.8 in), and you empty its bin by hand. If your sofa or bed sits low, the
**Roborock Saros 10R** is the only robot here close to 3 in tall, and it had the best
obstacle avoidance in our sources. It costs about four times as much.

## How we picked

We haven't tested these robots ourselves. Every score on this page comes from public
evidence: hands-on reviews by Tom's Guide, TechRadar, T3, TechGearLab, Vacuum Wars,
Expert Reviews, BGR, MacRumors, nextpit and netzwelt, plus the makers' spec sheets and
warranty terms, the CPSC recall database and USPTO trademark records. Each product's
review page lists every source with the date we checked it.

Each pick gets two separate numbers:

- **[Trust Score](/methodology/trust-score/)**: how safe the purchase is, from 0 to 100.
  It checks independent evidence, safety, how transparent the maker is, the brand and the
  warranty. It doesn't say which robot cleans best.
- **Small-space fit**: how well the robot works in a small home, from 0 to 100. It weighs
  footprint and clearance (40%), cleaning performance in independent tests (35%) and
  navigation in tight spaces (25%). Price never enters either number.

We left out robots with fewer than two independent hands-on reviews we could read, such as
the eufy Omni C20.

## What matters in a small apartment

- **Dock footprint.** Self-emptying docks range from 9 × 6.5 in (Tapo) to about
  15 × 19 in (eufy, Roborock Saros). Measure the spot before you buy: makers also ask for
  clear space in front of and beside the dock, about 4.9 ft in front and 1.6 ft on each
  side for the Tapo.
- **Height.** A robot that fits under the sofa cleans the dustiest spot in the room. Most
  budget robots are 3.8–3.9 in tall because of the LiDAR turret on top. The Saros 10R is
  about 3.1 in, and the eufy Omni E25 is 4.4 in.
- **Obstacle avoidance.** Small apartments mean cables, shoes and chair legs in every room.
  The budget robots here map well but avoid obstacles poorly, so plan to pick up before each
  run. The eufy (21 of 24) and the Saros 10R (24 of 24) did far better in Vacuum Wars' obstacle
  test.
- **Mapping matters less.** Multi-floor maps and room-by-room schedules are selling points
  for houses. In a studio, a reliable single map and a no-go zone or two are enough.

## Best budget robot vacuum for a small apartment

The **Tapo RV30 Max Plus** and the **Roborock Q7 M5** cost about the same, and both map
accurately with LiDAR. The difference is the dock. The Tapo's auto-empty dock means you
empty a 3 L bag now and then instead of a small bin every few runs. The Roborock's
charge-only dock is tiny, and Roborock sells a version with an auto-empty dock, the
Q7 M5+, which we haven't scored.

The Q7 M5 scores lower on trust (71) mainly because two independent reviewers, at
TechRadar and T3, hit the same app bugs: room names that disappeared and zones that
wouldn't save. TechRadar expected a fix; we'll re-check at the next review.

The **Dreame D10 Plus Gen 2** has the strongest hard-floor results of the three in Tom's
Guide's tests and a 3-year warranty on the robot and base, but it picked up only 40% of
the hair on low-pile carpet. Dreame now lists it as discontinued, so buy it only new and
only while stock lasts. Dreame's warranty covers purchases from its own store or its Amazon
US store.

## Robot vacuum and mop for a small apartment: is a full dock worth it?

An all-in-one dock empties the bin and washes and dries the mop, so you touch the robot
far less. The cost is floor space: the **eufy Omni E25** dock is 14.6 × 19.1 in and
17.3 in tall, about the size of a small nightstand, and the **Saros 10R** dock is similar
and weighs about 25 lb.

Pick the eufy if you have pets: it had some of the best pet-hair and anti-tangle results in
Vacuum Wars' tests. Pick the Saros 10R if low furniture or clutter is the problem. If you
don't have the floor space, the budget robots' basic mops are fine for light cleaning, but
none of them lift the mop over rugs.

## Quiet robot vacuum for an apartment

Independent noise figures are scarce and measured differently, so treat these as rough:

- TechGearLab measured the Dreame D10 Plus Gen 2 at 55.4 dB while vacuuming and 78 dB
  while the dock emptied it.
- Tom's Guide measured the Roborock Q7 M5 at 53–72 dB with a phone meter at 1 m, depending
  on floor and suction mode.
- Tapo rates the RV30 Max Plus at 52–55 dB; we found no independent measurement.
- MacRumors found the Saros 10R loud when vacuuming and quiet when mopping.

Self-emptying docks are loud while they empty. If you work from home in a studio, schedule
runs for when you're out.

## What we'd skip

The **iRobot Roomba 205 DustCompactor Combo** looks made for small homes: no dock tower and
a bin that compacts dirt. In [TechRadar's review](https://www.techradar.com/home/robot-vacuums/irobot-roomba-205-dustcompactor-combo-review),
it measured about 78 dB at 5 ft, the noisiest robot the reviewer had tested, its side
brush fell off twice and the app was buggy. We haven't scored it.

## Why every Safety score is 8 out of 20

All five robots lose the same 12 points on safety, for the same two reasons. We couldn't
verify a UL or ETL listing or an FCC ID for any of these models in a public database, and
none of the makers declare materials on their product pages or spec sheets. None of the brands has a CPSC recall for robot
vacuums in the last five years. If a maker sends us certification records, we'll update the
score and note the change.
