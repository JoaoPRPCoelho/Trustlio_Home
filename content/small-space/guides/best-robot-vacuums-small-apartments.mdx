## How to choose a robot vacuum for a small apartment

This is placeholder copy. The real guide starts with a direct answer, then explains the
three things that matter most in a small space:

- **Height.** Under 3.5 in fits beneath most low sofas and bed frames.
- **Dock footprint.** Self-emptying docks are convenient but take floor space you may not have.
- **Noise.** In a studio, the vacuum runs in the room you live in.
