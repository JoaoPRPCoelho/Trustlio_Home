## The short answer

For most studios up to about 400 sq ft, get the **Levoit Vital 200S**. It's rated at 250 cfm,
HouseFresh's measurements match that rating, and three independent reviews agree it cleans
fast. Its Trust Score is 92, with medium confidence.

If your bed is a few feet from the purifier, the **Coway Airmega Mighty2** costs more but
cleaned the most air at quiet fan speeds of the conventional purifiers here, and it has no
ionizer and no app to manage. If your studio is under about 250 sq ft, the **Levoit Eleva
300X** is smaller and cheaper, with a Trust Score of 87, though only two reviews exist so
far because it's new.

## How we picked

We haven't tested these purifiers ourselves. Every score on this page comes from public
evidence: hands-on reviews by HouseFresh, Air Purifier First, BreatheSafeAir, Live Science,
Reviewed, Trusted Reviews, Ideal Home, House Digest and SmartWorld, plus the makers' spec
sheets and warranty terms, the California Air Resources Board's list of certified air
cleaners, the CPSC recall database and USPTO trademark records. Each product's review page
lists every source with the date we checked it.

Noise and quiet-speed figures lean on HouseFresh, which measures every unit in the same
728 cu ft room with the same meter at 3 ft. That makes the numbers comparable with each
other, but not with the makers' dB claims, which are usually lower.

Each pick gets two separate numbers:

- **[Trust Score](/methodology/trust-score/)**: how safe the purchase is, from 0 to 100.
  It checks independent evidence, safety, how transparent the maker is, the brand and the
  warranty. It doesn't say which purifier cleans best.
- **Small-space fit**: how well the purifier works in a studio, from 0 to 100. It weighs room
  coverage for a 400 sq ft studio (40%), how much air it cleans while staying under 45 dBA
  (35%) and floor footprint (25%). Price never enters either number.

## What matters in a studio

- **Clean air delivery rate (CADR).** CADR is how many cubic feet of filtered air a purifier
  delivers per minute. AHAM, the appliance makers' association that certifies these numbers,
  suggests a smoke CADR of at least two-thirds of the floor area. A 400 sq ft studio with
  8 ft ceilings needs about 267 cfm; a 250 sq ft one needs about 167. Measure your space
  (length × width) before you buy.
- **Noise at the speed you'll actually use.** In a studio, the purifier runs in the room where
  you sleep and work. A unit that's quiet only on its lowest setting cleans very little air.
  We score how much air each one cleans while staying under 45 dBA at 3 ft, using HouseFresh's
  measurements.
- **Floor space.** The purifiers here take from 76 sq in (Core 300S) to about 159 sq in
  (AirFanta). Most need a few inches of clearance around the intake, so leave room around them.
- **Ozone.** California requires every air cleaner sold there to be certified by the state
  for electrical safety and ozone emissions. None of the picks uses an ozone generator. The
  Winix 5510's PlasmaWave ionizer can be switched off.

## Best air purifier for a small studio

If your studio is under about 250 sq ft, or the purifier only has to cover a sleeping
alcove, you don't need a 250 cfm unit. The **Levoit Eleva 300X** has an AHAM-verified CADR
of 161 cfm, measured at 165 by HouseFresh, in a 9.4 in cylinder. It uses separate HEPA and
carbon filters, so you replace the cheaper carbon filter more often and the HEPA filter
less often (Levoit rates it for up to two years).

The **Levoit Core 300S** is smaller still at 8.7 in across and 5.95 lb, light enough for a
shelf or nightstand. Three reviews over months of use back it, but it's rated for about
220 sq ft, HouseFresh measured 126 cfm on smoke against Levoit's 141, and only its lowest
speed stays under 45 dBA. For a whole studio, the Eleva 300X costs less and cleans more.

## Quiet air purifier for sleeping in a studio

Every purifier here is quiet on its lowest setting, at about 35–39 dBA at 3 ft in
HouseFresh's tests. What differs is how much air each one cleans at that noise level:

- **AirFanta 3Pro:** 213 cfm under 45 dBA
- **Coway Airmega Mighty2:** 151 cfm at 42.6 dBA (Speed 2)
- **Levoit Vital 200S:** 128 cfm under 45 dBA
- **Winix 5510:** 125 cfm under 45 dBA
- **Levoit Eleva 300X:** 115 cfm under 45 dBA
- **Levoit Core 300S:** only Speed 1 (38.9 dBA) stays under 45 dBA

At top speed, the Winix 5510 is the loudest here at 67.2 dBA at 3 ft. Run any of them on high
while you're out or cooking, and on a low speed at night.

## Is a PC-fan purifier like the AirFanta worth it?

The **AirFanta 3Pro** moves air with four computer fans instead of one large blower, which is
why it cleans so much air at low noise. All three reviewers measured it well above the
conventional purifiers here, and it has the highest Small-space fit (90).

Its Trust Score is 68, the lowest here, and the gap is about the company, not the machine. AirFanta's
website gives an email address but no postal address, its US trademark application from
2024 isn't confirmed as registered, and the 1-year warranty has no published terms. It also
has no app, sensor or auto mode, and it looks like a piece of lab equipment. It was out of
stock at AirFanta's US store when we checked. If those trade-offs don't bother you, nothing
else here cleans as much air per dollar.

## Running costs

Filters cost more over a few years than the purifier itself. At list prices when we checked,
from the makers or the reviewers' figures:

- **Levoit Vital 200S:** $49.99 filter, about once a year (HouseFresh found the carbon often
  runs out within about six months)
- **Coway Airmega Mighty2:** $69.99 filter set, once a year (HouseFresh)
- **Winix 5510:** about $80 a year (HouseFresh)
- **Levoit Eleva 300X:** $39.99 HEPA filter, up to every two years, plus a carbon filter from
  $19.99 every 6–12 months
- **Levoit Core 300S:** $29.99 filter every 6–8 months
- **AirFanta 3Pro:** $54 for a basic filter set every 6–12 months (BreatheSafeAir)

Electricity is minor at the speeds you'd use overnight: the reviewers measured all of these
at about 2–10 W on their low settings.

## What we'd skip

- The **Shark NeverChange HP152** is cheap to run, but HouseFresh measured just 88 cfm at top speed and 52 cfm under 45 dBA. That's too little for most studios.
- The **Molekule Air Mini+** had poor PM1 removal in HouseFresh's tests and was loud at top
  speed.

We haven't scored either.

## Why every Safety score is 16 out of 20

None of these brands has a CPSC recall in the last five years, and all six purifiers are on
California's public list of certified air cleaners, which requires electrical safety testing.
We give credit only for a certification we can find in a public database, and each product's
review page lists its executive order number. All six lose the same 4 points because none of
the makers declares materials on its product page.
